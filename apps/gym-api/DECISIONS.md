# Gym API: defaults chosen where the plan was silent

- **Family and corporate rates** are separate plans (`kind`), not group billing. One membership per member.
- **Freezes** move the membership end date when the freeze is recorded (not when it ends), so the end date shown is always correct. Ending a freeze early gives back the unused days. A freeze must start today or later, inside the membership dates, and cannot overlap another freeze. A membership that already has a renewal after it cannot be frozen (freeze or cancel the renewal instead), so memberships never overlap.
- **Renewal** starts the day after the latest non-cancelled membership ends, or today if that has passed. A chosen start date that overlaps another membership is rejected.
- **Complimentary memberships** need a reason and a manager (OWNER or GYM_MANAGER).
- **Payments** are recorded at sale time with today's date; any positive amount is accepted (discounts are allowed). Receipt numbers are `G-YYYY-NNNNNN`, sequential per year with no gaps.
- **Check-in**: duplicate window is 5 minutes after an allowed visit; a duplicate is logged with result DUPLICATE and is not a visit. Unknown, tampered and old-version codes are logged as DENIED with UNKNOWN_CODE. A member with status INACTIVE is denied with INACTIVE_MEMBER. Overrides need a manager and a reason of at least 3 characters; they are flagged on the check-in row and written to the audit log.
- **No offline check-in queue**: if the front desk loses connection, the screen must show "no connection" and staff use manual lookup when it returns. Recorded as a risk.
- **QR codes** hold only `memberId:version`, signed with HMAC-SHA256 using `SESSION_SECRET`. Rotating `SESSION_SECRET` invalidates all codes (and sessions).
- **Member logins** come only from invitations and need an email on the member record. The invite link is also returned to the desk so it can be shared in person.
- **Classes**: weekday 0 = Sunday, start time in Addis Ababa time. Sessions are generated 28 days ahead whenever a template is created and by the daily job. Booking needs an ACTIVE (not frozen) membership on the class date. Members and front desk can cancel until 2 hours before; after that only a manager with a reason. Cancelled bookings can be re-booked.
- **Trainers** see members assigned to them (and their photos and visits), their own classes and rosters, and their own personal sessions.
- **Reminders** (each can be switched off in settings; all on by default): renewal reminder `expiringDays` (default 3) before the end; expired message the day after the end (only for memberships that ended in the last 7 days, so old data never triggers a flood); "we miss you" after `inactiveDays` (default 14) without a visit, once per period of inactivity (counted from the last visit, or the membership start if never visited); birthday greeting on the day (29 February birthdays on 28 February in non-leap years). Only members with consent receive them; quiet hours 20:00 to 07:00 Addis time are handled by MessageService.
- **Announcement cap**: 4 marketing messages per member in any 30 days, counted from when they were sent (or scheduled, during quiet hours). Members over the cap are counted as `capped` on the campaign.
- **Dashboard**: "active members" counts members with a non-cancelled membership covering today (frozen included). Revenue counts VALID payments by `paidAt` in the month; payments not linked to a membership are shown as "Other". Attendance uses ALLOWED check-ins in the last 30 days, Addis time. At-risk excludes members whose membership is frozen today.
- **Dashboard access**: only OWNER and GYM_MANAGER (front desk uses `/memberships/expiring` instead), so revenue never reaches trainers or front desk.
- **Scheduler** runs every 15 minutes (first run 10 seconds after start) when `SCHEDULER_ENABLED=true`; every step is idempotent.
- **bcryptjs** is listed under devDependencies in package.json (copied from property-api); `src/seed.ts` needs it at runtime, which works in development because `@platform/nest-common` depends on it. Move it to dependencies the next time the lockfile is updated.
